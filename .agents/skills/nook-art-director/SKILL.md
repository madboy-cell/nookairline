# Sky Party — Master Art Direction & Visual Guide

Use this skill whenever designing, drafting prompts, or generating **costume character sprites (Mythic, Epic, Rare)** or **Destination Cabin Sceneries** (Sky Lounge panoramic backdrops) in Sky Party.

---

## 1. The Cardinal Art Direction Rule: 2.8-Heads-Tall Super-Cute Chibi

> [!CRITICAL]
> **ALL IN-GAME COSTUMES AND TOKENS MUST BE 2.8-TO-3 HEADS TALL SUPER-CUTE CHIBI ANIME.**
> Never generate tall, realistic, or 7–8 heads tall adult anime proportions. Realistic adult anatomy breaks the entire game's visual consistency across Monopoly board tiles, pedestal wardrobes, and couple lounge seats.
>
> **Every character sprite must embody**:
> * **Proportions**: 2.8 to 3 heads tall, adorable compact chibi body, cute expressive hands and feet.
> * **Face**: **100% human anime face** (NEVER animal muzzle or furry face), large luminous jewel-like eyes, soft round cheeks with rosy blush.
> * **Lineart & Color**: Crisp, dark cell-shaded anime contours, saturated and vibrant mobile gacha game palette, rich lighting.
> * **Background**: Isolated on **pure flat solid white background (`#FFFFFF`)**, no floor shadow, no floor grid, no decorative border or device frame.

## 2. The Couple Blueprint (Kemonomimi DNA)

Outfits in Sky Party are always designed in **romantically harmonized, symmetrical couple pairs** sharing a signature season theme:

### A. Male Character (`bkk`) — *The Charismatic Wolf Boy Prince* 🐺
* **Face & Hair**: Dashing, charming human anime boy face with playful or noble smile, tiny cute fang, tousled styled anime hair.
* **Kemonomimi Accents**:
  - Soft, fluffy silver, slate, or theme-matching wolf ears on top of hair (accented with gold/silver ear-cuffs).
  - Magnificent, fluffy, expressive bushy wolf tail (often tied with ribbons or glowing with elemental tips).
* **Attire & Props**: Tailored gala coats, knightly cuirasses, double-breasted vests, or royal tunics; holding weapons, scepters, or instruments (e.g. conductor baton, trident, spear, oar, khopesh).

### B. Female Character (`dxb`) — *The Enchanting Dragon Girl Princess* 🐉
* **Face & Hair**: Sweet, breathtaking human anime girl face with large luminous eyes, soft rosy blushing cheeks, flowing styled hair with braids or pins.
* **Kemonomimi Accents**:
  - Small, translucent pearlescent dragon horns with gold filigree rings.
  - Slender, graceful dragon tail with crystal, feather, or jewel accents.
  - Optional sheer gossamer dragon/fairy wings matching the gown.
* **Attire & Props**: Haute-couture ballgowns, corset bodices, layered feather/lace ruffles, high-low hems; holding delicate royal props (e.g. masquerade fan, mirror shield, glowing lantern, tambourine, spellbook).

## 5. Destination Cabin Scenery Direction (Zero Humans)

Cabin sceneries serve as the romantic atmospheric backdrop for the Sky Lounge where the couple's avatars sit, chat, and cuddle.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **THE ZERO-HUMAN RULE FOR SCENERIES**
> **ABSOLUTELY NO PEOPLE, NO HUMANS, NO FIGURES, NO SILHOUETTES IN FRAME!**
> If an image generates a person sitting on the furniture, it is an automatic failure. The seating must be completely vacant and awaiting the player avatars.

### The 3-Layer Spatial Composition:
1. **Foreground Stage**: Lavish, empty seating for two (curved plush velvet sofa, low mahogany/marble table with softly glowing lanterns/crystals, polished marble/sandstone floor reflecting the lighting).
2. **Midground Architecture**: Panoramic curved floor-to-ceiling glass dome, arched crystalline Gothic colonnade, or open-air palace balustrade.
3. **Background Wonder**: Breathtaking landmark (e.g. radiant spiral galaxy, dancing Aurora Borealis over alpine glaciers, illuminated sunken Atlantis coral palaces, Great Pyramids at Nile sunset, Venetian Grand Canal under moonlight).

### Scenery Output Formats:
* **Portrait (9:16)**: `sX_cabin_portrait` (Mobile fullscreen view)
* **Widescreen (16:9)**: `sX_cabin_wide` (Desktop lounge view)
* **Thumbnail (1:1)**: `sX_cabin_thumb` (UI selector cards)
