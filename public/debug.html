How to read it. Conceal % = share of audio the phone had to invent because packets were missing (this is the robot sound). Stretch % = audio sped up / slowed down to resync a late or bursty stream. Loss % = packets that never arrived. JBuf = how much audio the phone is holding to smooth jitter. Path shows whether the call runs on relay/udp, relay/tcp or relay/tls. Bars: green < 1%, amber 1–5%, red ≥ 5% concealment. Open the call with ?debug=1 on a phone to get the 🤖 Robot now button.
